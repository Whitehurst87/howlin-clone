Our Story

Welcome to the Den

"A tribute to a bygone era, reimagined for the modern night owl."

Nestled in the historic Tower District, The Howlin' Wolf is more than just a bar—it's an escape. We blend the speakeasy mystique of the jazz age with an eccentric, rock 'n' roll edge.

Whether you're here for a perfectly balanced Old Fashioned, live local music, or just to soak in the art-deco vibes, we've saved a seat for you.

Established in 2021, our space evokes the glamour of big band nights mixed with a dash of whimsy. Think tuxedos meeting tattoos. Our walls feature café racer art and eccentric portraits that watch over you as you sip.

The Team: Our bartenders are craftsmen and craftswomen, obsessed with the details—from house-made shrubs to hand-carved ice.
